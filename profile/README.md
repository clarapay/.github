# ClaraPay

**AI patient billing and collections, from early-out through recovery.**

ClaraPay is an AI-first patient billing and collections partner for healthcare
providers and creditors of every kind. We work balances from the first
statement through collections, on contingency: you pay only when we collect.

- **Early-out billing:** provider-branded, pre-charge-off patient outreach
- **Collections:** voice AI, email, SMS and mail, with compliance built into every touch
- **Patient portal:** secure card and ACH payments and payment plans, self-serve
- **Reporting:** real-time visibility into placements, payments and remittance
