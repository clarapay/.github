# ClaraPay

**The financial infrastructure for healthcare: one platform from first statement to final settlement.**

ClaraPay is a single system for how healthcare gets paid: the patient's bill, the
practice's revenue cycle, and the collections that follow. Every action runs on one
compliance engine that enforces FDCPA, Reg F, TCPA, FCRA, HIPAA and state rules in
code, not in a policy binder.

## What we do

- **Collections:** third-party recovery with a collector desk, Clara AI voice, SMS,
  email, letters and a patient portal, all running under a compliance state machine
- **Patient portal:** bills, saved payment methods, self-service plans, disputes,
  hardship, messaging and receipts, in English and Spanish
- **Early-out billing:** pre-collection patient billing under the practice's own brand,
  with automatic hand-off to collections at the configured aging threshold
